Two tries per person, same instruction, different dice. Each result then goes through the same cut-out and hall as before. Does this pass?

About 14 seconds per crown on your card. If it passes, the rule is: a crown is never a layer; a crowned king is a second painted version of his own bust, baked for the ruler pool only. Backgrounds stay layers.
