Sea King · brand plan · 2026-10-23

Sea King in House Hansen

The game works. It is dressed as a different brand. Every screen is black and gold, the two things the House Hansen rules exclude first. The fix is mostly a token swap, a type change and a shorter map, with one real design job: a child mark for the game.

What I looked at: all thirteen screens at phone width, live from the published site. Setup, the main screen top to bottom, Landfall, the fight, Plunder or Stay, the land summary, the winter market, a birth card, the fork at 25, the Saga, the Heir, the Oath and the coronation.

What I read: the brand repo as of this morning. Palette and tokens, the decisions log through the G08 lock, the four look pieces, the site mockup, the backlog and the design landscape.

Plan

Six phases, ordered by what constrains the rest

Each phase ships on its own and lands on your phone at the next open. Nothing in the GDD changes. One screen, three phases, press Sail.

  1. Tokens and the two grounds

    one commitwhat you see: the whole game in oat by day and Forge by night
    • Copy the brand's token file into the game, dated, with the source path. The game must stay self-contained for offline play.
    • Replace every literal colour in the stylesheet with a token, using the palette map above. Light values on the root, dark values under the phone's dark setting and under a manual toggle.
    • Top band and map become deep fjord with oat type in both themes.
    • Three first-play fixes while I am in the file: the map is cropped to the known coasts and capped at under half the screen so Home is one swipe away; the odds label reads "sure, likely, even, narrow, long" instead of a percentage that said 100 and then bled; a holding taken this summer is not contested until next summer, since the jarl who stayed just won it.

  2. Type

    one commitwhat you see: the year in a real serif, numbers that line up
    • Fraunces for the year, names, card titles and the result line. Hanken Grotesk for body, buttons and labels. JetBrains Mono with tabular figures for every stat, count and price.
    • Self-hosted woff2 subsets in the repo and in the service worker cache, about 120 KB. Google Fonts would break the first offline open.
    • A five-step scale and nothing outside it. Labels at .16em.

  3. Components

    one commitwhat you see: the sketch above, on every card
    • Cards at 2 px radius with a one-pixel rule, no fill difference heavier than oat to oat light.
    • Buttons: woad fill with oat light type for the one action, rule-only for the rest. The Sail button keeps its full width.
    • The strength bar: woad against chestnut on an oat-line track, 6 px tall, the same bar on ship, land and Oath.
    • Tags: pine for kin and sons, muted for King and titles. The native select for destination and marriage styled to the same rule and radius.
    • Scene cards become a bottom sheet on the phone, rising from the Sail button, with the sticky band still visible above.

  4. The map and motion

    one sessionwhat you see: fog that actually lifts
    • Sea in deep fjord. Known coasts as oat dots, holdings in woad lifted, the home hall in auburn light, dark coasts hinted in iron so the world has an edge before you reach it. Sea lanes at the thin strand weight.
    • Landfall: the three revealed coasts fade in over 600 ms. The fight: the bar fills to the result over 600 ms, then the line appears. The sheet rises over 300 ms. Easing out, never a bounce. All instant under reduced motion.
    • The year numeral in the band crossfades on Sail. Slow and heavy, as the brand guesses its motion should be.

  5. Mark, icons, imprint

    design rounds at your pacewhat you see: an app icon that belongs to the house
    • A child mark for Sea King built from the same geometry as G08: the golden spiral generator, the thin strand at 6.4 on 240, the size rule below 48 and 24 px. Subject in the calls below. It goes on the approval board like the H did.
    • Eight icons at the strand weight: ship, hall, holding, silver, men, wanderer, death, fork. Used sparingly, next to the section labels and on the scene titles. The brand backlog has wanted this set; the game is the first place that needs it.
    • "House Hansen" once, small, under the setup screen. Nowhere else, per the naming rule.
    • New 192 and 512 icons, favicon and touch icon cut by the same export script the site mock uses.

  6. Texture and the side-by-side

    half a sessionwhat you see: wool, not plastic
    • One SVG grain on the page ground at low opacity, never on cards or the map. If it reads as noise on your phone it comes out.
    • Screenshots of every scene at 375 and 430 wide next to the four look pieces. Anything that would not sit on the same wall gets fixed.
    • Record the radius, type and icon decisions back in the brand repo's decisions log, since the game is the first app and closes three open backlog items.
Your calls

Four decisions that are taste, with my pick on each

Say "go" and I take every recommendation. Overrule any one by name.

Serif Fraunces

All four look pieces you approved use Fraunces. The site mock switched to Newsreader two days later and nothing records why. Fraunces has more character at the sizes a phone uses for a year numeral. Switching later is one line.

Default ground Follow the phone

Oat by day, Forge at night, with a toggle in the Dynasty drawer. The alternative is Forge only, which fits a game played in bed but drops the Longhouse base the brand is built on.

Child mark subject The arm ring

The repo is ring-giver, the first title is Ring-giver, the game is about silver, and the silver arm ring is already in the brand's jewelry rules. A ring whose two terminals are the G08 spiral curls makes it a child of the H by construction. The other candidate is a ship stem with the same curl.

Radius 2 px

The brand's open question is rounded Longhouse against square Northern. The site mock already went to 2 px and it reads as cut wood. Taking it here closes the question for apps.

Not changing

What stays exactly as it is

  • Every rule in the GDD and every TODO recommendation already taken. This is dress, not mechanics.
  • The single screen, the section order, the map as a picture with no interaction.
  • The copy. Short declaratives are already the "well groomed" half of the thesis.
  • No runes, no interlace on screen. Urnes belongs to the mark and the cover, per card 2.
