Ring Giver · playtest round 1 · 24 October 2026

Eight Players, Twenty Summers

Eight Opus agents, each with a persona (six grand-strategy players, two from outside the genre), played Ring Giver on a 390 × 844 phone through the real screens for twenty game-years or until the line broke, logging what they felt as they went. The panel agrees: the numbers are honest, the ledger and the Plunder-or-stay sheet are the best screens, and the people are the reason to come back. It also agrees on what hurts. One winter card killed three dynasties in year six with no number on it; bought men never got on the boat; the big moments happen in grey ledger lines; and from year seven there is nothing to fear and nothing to buy.

Two bugs found before anyone played, both fixed

Driving the real screens by touch turned up two things the headless harnesses never could. Coasts could not be tapped on the map: the chip strip over the water covered the whole chart and took every touch (one line of CSS; service worker bumped to v11). The King froze after any reload: the save holds him twice and loading parted the copies, so after closing the app his age, stats, place and death roll stopped moving; migrate re-links him. Both are in the working tree, not yet committed.

What the panel hit

blockerThe winter trapdoor: Guard 0, the King slain, the line broken

8 of 8 players (2 outside the genre) · 38 notes

Three of the eight dynasties ended in winter 799, six years in, the same way: a rival sail came up the fjord, the card said Guard 0 with forty to seventy men in the hall, the King died in his door, and his son was four. Nothing before that winter says what the guard is made of (a named man at home), that a strange sail can kill the King, or that a King who dies before a son is sixteen ends the dynasty, which is true for every death before 809. Every summer screen prices the King's risk to a tenth of a percent; the one risk that ended the run had no number anywhere. The careful players (the min-maxer, the Paradox veteran, the history teacher) were the ones it killed, because they had read "no risk to him at these odds" and believed the ship was the dangerous place.

  • But it came without the warning I had in 805 ('Ælla's fleet is gathering… sails next winter'). In 806 the threat fizzled silently, so I'd learned it was empty; five years on it lands with no clock. 'Guard 0' — I had 10 men unled at home and never knew they didn't count as guard. majorNumbers where a sentence should be

majorYear seven: nothing to fear, nothing to want

Small bugs and slips the panel found

  • The rumor's 20 silver is missing from the autumn ledger, so the sum is off by 20 (three players did the arithmetic).
  • Two "Rent · Birka" lines in one autumn when a king took the coast back.
  • "1 men", stray spaces before commas on ruler cards ("Birka (his seat) , Gotland ."), the "Hall · 1" badge that never clears.
  • "His realm: 0 of 1 coasts held" on a two-coast realm; "Hordaland lies open to be held" when it is a fight.
  • Tapping a king's card on the setup screen starts the game at once; the custom-king steppers reset between taps, so a custom build could not be made.
  • The end card says "No son, no son-in-law" when there is a son of four; the coronation card reads "Same screen. Smaller kingdom." to a new king.
  • After a succession every jarl showed "Kin" (one player); the Saga shows a modern ISO date ("the seas of 2026-10-25").
  • Spring reopens on last year's target even when it is now "hopeless", and names the coast aimed at, not the one reached.
